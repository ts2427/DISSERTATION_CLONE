Item 8.01 – Other Information.

On February 5, 2013, Dell Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Denali Holding Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Parent”), Denali Intermediate Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent (“Intermediate”), and Denali Acquiror Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Intermediate (“Merger Sub” and, together with Parent and Intermediate, the “Parent Parties”), providing for the merger of Merger Sub with and into the Company (the “Merger”), with the Company surviving the Merger as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Intermediate. Parent is owned by Michael Dell, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company and investment funds affiliated with Silver Lake Partners. The material terms of the Merger Agreement, including the conditions to the proposed Merger, will be described in a subsequent filing on Form 8-K.

A copy of the press release issued by the Company announcing the proposed Merger is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and is incorporated by reference in this Item 8.01.

In addition, on February 5, 2013 the Company engaged in various communications with employees, investors and other persons concerning the proposed Merger. Copies of those communications are filed as exhibits to this report.

Additional Information and Where to Find It

This filing may be deemed solicitation material in respect of the proposed acquisition of the Company of Mr. Dell and Silver Lake Partners and their respective affiliates. In connection with the proposed merger transaction, the Company will file with the SEC and furnish to the Company’s stockholders a proxy statement and other relevant documents.

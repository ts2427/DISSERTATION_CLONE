Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

On January 21, 2008, Denis J. O’Leary joined the board of directors of Fiserv, Inc. He will serve on the audit committee of the board.

Mr. O’Leary, 51, is a consultant in the enterprise technology, financial services, and consumer payments industries both directly, and, since 2006, as a senior advisor to The Boston Consulting Group. He is also a private investor primarily focusing on private, early stage companies. Through early 2003, Mr. O’Leary spent 25 years at J.P. Morgan Chase & Company in various capacities, including director of finance, chief information officer, head of retail branch banking, managing executive of Chase.com/Lab Morgan, and, from 1994 to 2003, Executive Vice President. Since 2003, Mr. O’Leary has served on the board of directors of McAfee, Inc.

Item 5.02.   Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

Fiserv, Inc. (the “Company”) filed a Current Report on Form 8-K on August 1, 2023 (the “Original Form 8-K”) reporting, under Item 5.02, the appointment of Charlotte Yarkoni to its board of directors. Because Ms. Yarkoni was not appointed to serve on any committee of the board of directors at the time the Original Form 8-K was filed, disclosure of the committees of the board of directors on which she would serve was not included in accordance with Instruction 2 to Item 5.02. The Company hereby amends Item 5.02 of the Original Form 8-K to include information on the committee assignments. Other than providing the additional information, no other disclosure in the Original Form 8-K is amended by this Form 8-K/A.

On February 21, 2024, the board of directors of the Company appointed Ms. Yarkoni to the Audit and Risk committees of the board of directors effective as of February 21, 2024.

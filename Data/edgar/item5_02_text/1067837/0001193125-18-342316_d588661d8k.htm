Item 5.02.

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(b)    Principal Accounting Officer Retiring

On December 3, 2018, Entercom Communications Corp. (the “Company”) announced that, in connection with his pending retirement, Eugene D. Levin will be stepping down as Principal Accounting Officer and Controller, effective December 31, 2018.

(c)    Appointment of Interim Principal Accounting Officer

On December 3, 2018, the Company also announced that it has appointed James Hamill (age 38), the Company’s Assistant Controller and Treasurer, to serve as the interim Principal Accounting Officer and Controller effective January 1, 2019. Prior to joining Entercom, Mr. Hamill spent eight years with KPMG, last serving as a Senior Manager, and several years as the Assistant Controller for a large public company in the energy sector. He is a CPA and a graduate of Wake Forest University. Mr. Hamill does not have an employment agreement with the Company.

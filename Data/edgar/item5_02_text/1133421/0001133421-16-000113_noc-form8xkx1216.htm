Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

      (d)  Election of New Director

On December 7, 2016, the Board of Directors of Northrop Grumman Corporation (the “Company”) elected Mark A. Welsh III to the Board of Directors effective December 8, 2016. General Welsh is the Dean of the Bush School of Government and Public Service at Texas A&M University, a position he has held since August 2016. Prior to his current position, General Welsh most recently served as the Chief of Staff, U.S. Air Force from August 2012 until his retirement in July 2016, and Commander, U.S. Air Forces in Europe; Commander, Air Component Command, Ramstein Air Base, Germany; and Director, Joint Air Power Competency Center, Ramstein Air Base, Germany from December 2010 to July 2012. He served on active duty in the U.S. Air Force for almost 40 years in various positions.
 
The Board of Directors appointed General Welsh to the Audit Committee and the Policy Committee.

General Welsh is entitled to receive an annual cash retainer of $122,500 per year as well as an additional $10,000 retainer for serving on the Audit Committee. In addition, General Welsh will receive an annual equity grant of $145,000 in deferred stock units to be paid out at the conclusion of his board service, or earlier, as specified by General Welsh. General Welsh’s retainer fees and equity grant will be prorated for 2016.

On December 7, 2016, the Board of Directors also approved an increase in the size of the Board from 13 members to 14 members.

A copy of the Company’s press release announcing the election of General Welsh is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.

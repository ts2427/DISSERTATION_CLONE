Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On March 5, 2013, Facebook, Inc. (the “Company”) filed a Current Report on Form 8-K disclosing, among other things, that Susan Desmond-Hellmann was appointed to the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) effective March 5, 2013. At the time of the appointment, the Board had not decided on which of the Board’s standing committees Dr. Desmond-Hellmann would serve.
On April 2, 2013, the Board appointed Dr. Desmond-Hellmann to the Audit Committee of the Board, effective May 15, 2013. Peter A. Thiel will move from the Audit Committee to the Compensation Committee of the Board, effective as of the same date.

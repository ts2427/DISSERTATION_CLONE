Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers, Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangement for Certain Officers.

On August 12, 2020, the Compensation Committee (the “Committee”) of the Board of Directors of Alpha and Omega Semiconductor Limited (the “Company”) approved an incentive bonus plan (the “Plan”) pursuant to which each of Mike F. Chang, our chief executive officer, Yifan Liang, our Chief Financial Officer and Corporate Secretary, Yueh-Se Ho, our Chief Operating Officer, Stephen Chang, our Executive Vice President Product Line Management and Bing Xue, our Senior Vice President of Sales will be eligible to receive an incentive award based on the level of attainment of specified Company performance goals. Under the Plan, the performance goals are comprised of a range of non-GAAP earnings per share and revenue for calendar year 2020. Assuming the minimum performance goals are achieved, the actual amount of the award earned by an executive officer will range from 25% to 50% of annual base salary for our chief executive officer and from 15% to 30% of annual base salary for our other executive officers depending on the level of attainment of the performance goals. Any earned bonus will be paid by March 15, 2021 in cash, Company common shares or a combination thereof as determined by the Committee subject to the executive officer’s continued service through the payment date.

The description above is only a summary and qualified in its entirety by the Plan, a copy of which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ending September 30, 2020 to be filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

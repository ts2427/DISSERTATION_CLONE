Item 5.02.            Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

On August 23, 2022, Stephen Adams informed the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Camping World Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) that he would be retiring from the Board, effective September 30, 2022.  Mr. Adams has been a director of the Company since its formation and has been a major contributor and influence to the recreational vehicle industry.  He has contributed to the growth and awareness of both the Good Sam and Camping World brands over several decades.

 

In recognition of Mr. Adams’ contributions and commitment to the industry and his long-standing counsel and dedication to the Company, the Board of Directors has provided Mr. Adams with the honorary title of Director Emeritus, a title which will not entitle Mr. Adams to compensation or voting rights with respect to any matters to be voted on by the Board of Directors.

 

In connection with Mr. Adams’ resignation, the Board reduced its size to seven directors, effective September 30, 2022.

Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On April 5, 2007, Motorola, Inc. (the “Company”) granted to Gregory Q. Brown, President and Chief Operating Officer of the Company, 400,000 options (vesting in four equal annual installments beginning on April 5, 2008) and 350,000 restricted stock units (vesting in 50% increments on the thirty month and sixty month anniversary of the grant date). The grant of stock options and restricted stock units to Mr. Brown was made in the Company’s standard Form Of Restricted Stock Unit Agreement, Form Of Stock Option Consideration Agreement, and Form of Award Document, included as Exhibits 10.3, 10.4 and 10.5, respectively, to the Company’s 2006 Annual Report on Form 10-K filed on February 28, 2007.

#### 2. Obaydin, I. (2024)
**Title:** The unintended cost of data breach notification laws: Evidence from managerial bad news hoarding  
**Journal:** *Journal of Business Finance & Accounting* (Wiley)  
**DOI/URL:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jbfa.12794  
**Publication Period:** 2024 (PRIMARY)

**Summary:** Using staggered adoption of state breach notification laws as a natural experiment, this paper finds that mandatory disclosure mandates paradoxically increase stock price crash risk. The mechanism proposed is that managers engage in "bad news hoarding" — strategically withholding negative information — which accumulates until a sharp, sudden correction. This directly challenges the transparency-improvement rationale of disclosure regulation.

**Relevance to Hypotheses:**
- **H2 (FCC regulation impact on returns):** The paper's core finding — that disclosure mandates can *increase* price volatility — is directly relevant to H2; the FCC rule may have analogous effects
- **H5 (volatility/information asymmetry):** Crash risk is a specific form of asymmetric information cost; directly relevant
- **Overall utility:** Must-cite for any discussion of unintended consequences of disclosure regulation

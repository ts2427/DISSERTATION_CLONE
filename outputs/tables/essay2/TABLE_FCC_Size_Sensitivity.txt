================================================================================

Tests whether FCC regulatory penalty is driven by firm size or is robust
across the firm size distribution. FCC-regulated firms are significantly larger
(2.02x mean assets, p<0.0001), so we test whether the FCC effect persists
when comparing across size quartiles.

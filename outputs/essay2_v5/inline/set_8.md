The four cells form one family, and the BH-adjusted p applies the Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment within it; the smallest BH-adjusted CV3 p is .535. Panel B asks whether any cell shows the effect to be negligible. A cell does so only if its 90 percent confidence interval lies inside ±0.191, the smallest effect size of interest (SESOI) from Table 11; no cell's interval does. MDE80, the minimum detectable effect at 80 percent power, runs from 0.821 to 2.632, or 4.30 to 13.80 times SESOI. Panel C shows p-values from procedures that understate clustering, to document over-rejection when few treated parents sit in a cell: HC1 and HC3 give p below .05 in 3 of the four cells, and CV3 in none. Q1 is the smallest quartile.

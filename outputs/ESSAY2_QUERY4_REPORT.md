## Headline consequences (none soften)

1. **The rule is a floor, not a ceiling (A1, verified verbatim), and it does not bind (A2).** Treatment does not predict disclosure delay in level, log, any quantile, the KM curves, or the KS test. ZERO-DELAY CONTAMINATION acknowledged (34.8% of delays are exactly 0 — occurrence dates defaulting to notification dates): no compliance claim is made from the sub-floor share. On the clean non-zero subsample (N=217) the null holds — treated median 63d vs control 50d (directionally LONGER, floor-consistent, nothing significant: log-rank p=.089, KS p=.25, coefs' CIs span zero) — so the mechanism finding stands without the contaminated tail. The draft's deadline-pressure mechanism is dead; "the mandate leaves observed notification behavior unchanged, and market uncertainty is likewise unchanged" is the story the corrected data support.
2. **The gradient reversal is EXPLAINED, not merely demonstrated (B1/B2/B2b).** Reclassifying treatment alone (pre-dedup data held fixed) removes the small-firm spike (+7.65 → +0.13). Two candidate mechanisms fail: records-per-event does not rise with size (B2), and the misclassified set is not systematically smaller (B2b, p=.59 old data). The operative mechanism is OUTCOME-extremity plus small-firm concentration: the 31 SIC-treated-but-unregistered records (Aero Charter, WillScot Mobile Mini, Impact Mobile Home Communities, Johnson Matthey, Charter Next Generation, DISH, …) carry a mean volatility change of **+7.81 vs −1.45 for correctly-classified carriers (t=4.43, p=.0001)**, and their share of the treated cell peaks in Q1 (26% vs 0% in Q2). SIC codes injected non-carrier records with large positive volatility shocks into the small-firm treated cell; reclassification removes them and the gradient collapses. (Family-attribution caveat: the record-level Form 499 flag is a stated reconstruction; a few concordant-family records land in the manufactured cell.)
3. **Classification survives its audits (C1/C2).** The committed registry snapshot natively retains 11,767 deregistered filers with start/end dates; stage 4 assigned treatment as of breach date; independent re-verification: 102/102 treated events have a date-valid family filer record. No treated family matches only non-covered filer categories.

================================================================================
WHY SCM IS THE PRIMARY CAUSAL ID
================================================================================

1. ENDOGENEITY CONCERN:
   - OLS regression (-2.19%) may suffer from unobserved confounding
   - FCC status is exogenous (assigned by regulation), but firm characteristics
     that correlate with being FCC-regulated may drive returns independent of regulation

2. SCM ADVANTAGE:
   - Compares each FCC firm to a synthetic control of non-FCC firms
   - Weights non-FCC firms to match pre-treatment characteristics
   - Controls for ALL pre-treatment differences (observed and unobserved)
   - Isolates the causal effect of FCC regulation itself

3. RESULT DIFFERENCE:
   - SCM effect (-4.03%) > OLS effect (-2.19%)
   - Suggests OLS underestimates the true FCC penalty due to confounding bias
   - Non-FCC firms that are most comparable to FCC firms see smaller returns
   - This drives the SCM effect to be larger in magnitude

================================================================================
LIMITATIONS & CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. Synthetic Control Matching assumes:
   - Parallel trends (pre-treatment, confirmed with pre-2007 test)
   - No unmeasured confounders (plausible given exogenous FCC status)
   - Common support (checked: ample non-FCC comparison firms)

2. Sprint proxy recovery:
   - 13 breaches (31% of total) use T-Mobile stock data
   - Theoretically justified (same parent company post-2020)
   - Result robust to excluding these (n=38: same mean effect)
   - Conservative: could argue for n=38 if proxy concerns arise

3. Sample size:
   - n=41 firms is modest but standard for SCM applications
   - Effect confidence depends on variability across firms (σ=8.1%)
   - 95% CI: [-6.52%, -1.55%] excludes zero, supporting significance
